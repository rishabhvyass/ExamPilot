Focus read

Testing shows that bugs are present. It can never prove that they are absent.

The aim is to find defects early, while they are still cheap to fix.

Verification asks: are we building the product right?

Validation asks: are we building the right product?

Black-box tests look only at inputs and outputs, never the code.

White-box tests are designed from the code’s own structure.

Boundary values sit at the edges, where most mistakes hide.

Regression tests re-run old checks after every change.
